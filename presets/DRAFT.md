# Preset tasks: draft for review

Nothing here is in the database yet. Cut, add, or change anything, then approve.

How to read the Repeats column:
- "Every 3 months" is a **countdown**. It restarts from the day you finish it.
- "Every Apr and Oct" or "Every Tuesday" is **calendar**. It lands on those dates no matter when the last one was done. Calendar dates default to the 1st unless a day is shown.
- "Apr to Oct" is an **active window**. The task sleeps outside those months.
- "(skip)" means a missed one drops off instead of carrying over.
- Seasonal months assume a US four-season climate. They're defaults, and whoever adds the preset can change them.

Buy is the Amazon search that becomes the buy link. The family edits it to their size once (for example, "20x25x1 air filter").

Everyone who adds a preset sees every task checked and unchecks what doesn't apply.
